ELDORIA
THE SACRED CRYSTALS
Long ago, there was a peaceful kingdom called Eldoria. The people lived in harmony, and the land was full of life. One dark night, an evil sorcerer known as Dark Lord Vexar attacked the kingdom using forbidden magic. The king was killed, the land was destroyed, and darkness spread everywhere.

Before the kingdom fell, the ancient guardians hid five Sacred Crystals across the world. These crystals are the only hope to restore Eldoria.
