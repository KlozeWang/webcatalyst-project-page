How a Hacker Thinks

A stylized, educational strategy game about the phases of security thinking: observe carefully, reason about systems, and choose ethical, defensive actions to understand how attackers find weak spots.

How to play

Progress through three phases. Each round, pick the best defensive-thinking response to the scenario.

  • Mindset: spot assumptions, boundaries, and incentives.
  • Recon: identify what is visible and what should remain private.
  • Exploitation: recognize weak links and apply the safest response.

Goal

Earn insight points by choosing secure, ethical, and practical responses.

No real-world hacking instructions are included. The game focuses on awareness and prevention.

Tip: Reading between the lines is often more valuable than guessing.
