Making a Metro Exodus–Style Game in Unity

If you want a game that feels like Metro Exodus, the key is not copying it directly, but building the same pillars: atmospheric survival FPS, grounded gunplay, scarce resources, strong lighting, stealth, exploration, and narrative-driven survival.

Unity 2022/2023 LTS HDRP recommended Start with a vertical slice

What to Build First

Don’t start with the whole open world. Build one polished level that proves the core loop: explore, loot, manage ammo, fight enemies, repair gear, and survive.

1

Core loop

Explore an area → find supplies → survive a fight/stealth encounter → return to a safe point → upgrade gear.

2

First-person controller

Movement, crouch, sprint, head bob, interaction, ladder/climbing if needed, and weapon aiming.

3

Gunplay and survival systems

Ammo scarcity, reload animation, weapon jams, durability, medkits, filters for gas mask, and a simple inventory.

4

Atmosphere

Heavy fog, volumetric lighting, dirty materials, particle effects, audio reverb, and environmental storytelling.

Recommended Unity Stack

  • Render Pipeline: HDRP for realistic lighting and post-processing
  • Input: New Input System
  • Camera: Cinemachine
  • AI: NavMesh + custom vision/hearing
  • Dialogue: Ink or Yarn Spinner
  • Saving: JSON save system
  • Animation: Animator with layered states and IK

Feature Breakdown

Feature What it should do Priority
Movement Walk, sprint, crouch, lean, jump, stamina High
Weapons Semi-auto and automatic weapons, recoil, sway, reload, attachments High
Stealth Enemy vision cone, sound propagation, hiding in darkness High
Survival Ammo management, filters, crafting, medkits, weapon maintenance High
AI Patrol, investigate, search, attack, communicate High
World Linear story zones with open side paths, not a giant sandbox at first Medium
Vehicle/Boat Optional later, after the shooter foundations are complete Low

Best Development Plan

A

Prototype the controller

Get movement, camera, crouch, and interaction working in a graybox level.

B

Add one weapon

Build a single rifle with recoil, ammo, reload, and hit detection.

C

Add one enemy type

Make a patrol AI that hears noise and sees the player in light.

Important Design Notes

  • Atmosphere matters more than scale. A small, polished level can feel bigger than a huge unfinished world.
  • Lighting is half the game. Use contrast, fog, and shadows to sell the mood.
  • Sound is critical. Gunshots, distant enemies, wind, creaking metal, and ambient music all matter.
  • Keep resources limited. Scarcity creates tension.
  • Use handcrafted encounters. Metro-style games feel strongest with authored moments.
