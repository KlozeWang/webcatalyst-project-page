Wasteland Exodus

A lightweight browser prototype inspired by post-apocalyptic survival shooters.

  • Move: WASD
  • Aim: Mouse
  • Shoot: Left Click
  • Reload: R
  • Sprint: Shift (uses stamina)
  • Extract: Reach the train after timer and press E

Scavenge crates for ammo and medkits. Avoid mutant swarms and radiation zones.
