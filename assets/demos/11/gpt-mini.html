Shadow of Time: Echo Runner

A silent protagonist moves through a broken timeline. Every loop leaves a shadow echo that repeats your past actions exactly.

Use the echoes to stand on switches, hold bridges, distract guardians, and solve time-based puzzles.

Goal: Reach the glowing gate at the end of each level. Some levels contain enemies or a boss time-shard. Plan carefully.

Click or tap to begin. Audio will start after the first interaction.
