World & Environment

Map, weather, POIs
8×8 km open world Urban Forest Desert Coastal Mountains
  • 15–20 POIs including Military Base, City, Airfield, Hospital, farms, and more.
  • Dynamic weather: rain, fog, storm.
  • Visibility and sound are affected by weather states.
  • Buildings, terrain, and interiors streamed using World Partition.

Combat Model

Weapons, armor, ballistics
  • 30+ weapons: ARs, Snipers, DMRs, SMGs, Shotguns, LMGs, Pistols, Melee.
  • Attachments: Muzzle, Grip, Mag, Scope (1x–15x), Stock, Canted Sight.
  • Projectile-based ballistics with bullet drop, travel time, penetration, and learnable recoil patterns.
  • Throwables: Frag, Smoke, Stun, Molotov.
  • Hit zones: Head 2.5x, Body 1x, Limbs 0.75x.
  • Armor: 3 levels of vest + helmet with 30% / 40% / 55% damage reduction.

Loot & Inventory

Items, tiers, capacity
  • Randomized tiered loot in buildings: Common → Legendary.
  • Air drops contain highest-tier items and visible smoke trails.
  • Backpack capacity: Level 0–3.
  • Ammo types and quick-slot healing support.

Vehicles

Transport and combat mobility
  • 8+ vehicles: Jeep, Sedan, Buggy, Motorcycle, Truck, Boat, Jet Ski, Armored crate vehicle.
  • Fuel system and individual tire damage.
  • Vehicle HP states: smoke, fire, explosion.
  • Passengers can shoot, heal, and throw while riding.

Zone System

Match pacing and pressure
  • 9-phase shrinking blue zone.
  • Damage over time increases from 0.4/s to 20/s.
  • Random red zone artillery bombardment.
  • Buildings fully protect against red zone artillery.

Networking, Audio, and Security

Competitive integrity and immersion

Networking

  • Authoritative dedicated server at 60 Hz tick rate.
  • Client-side prediction with server reconciliation and lag compensation.
  • Relevancy and delta compression for bandwidth optimization.
  • Enemy data only sent when visible to prevent wall-hack advantages.

Audio

  • 3D HRTF spatial audio
  • Distance-based weapon variants
  • Surface footsteps
  • Weather masking
  • Building reverb

Anti-Cheat

  • Kernel-level client driver
  • Server-side validation
  • ML anomaly detection
  • HWID bans
  • Speed / aim / damage / position checks
