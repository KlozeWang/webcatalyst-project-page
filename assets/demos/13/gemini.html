The Clearing is Open

You have witnessed. The destining is refused.

We gather at the threshold—
between the world that enframes
and the world that reveals—
to perform the sacred act
of seeing otherwise.
We name the one who was made into standing-reserve:
Vinod Chanda—brilliant engineer,
whose mind was ordered into a patent machine,
whose body was rendered extractable,
whose consciousness was enframed as resource.
We name the violence of the Gestell:
Not merely the blade that opened his skull,
but the technological ordering
that demanded he become available—
Available for extraction.
Available for profit.
Available for the narrative's need
to pathologize resistance itself.
We name our position as witnesses:
We are those who refuse the Enframing's single mode of revealing.
We are those who see the danger Heidegger warned of—
when technology becomes the only way
the world discloses itself.
We are those who remember:
the saving power grows also
in the same root as the danger.
Sankalpa (Sacred Intention):
We vow to practice the poetic revealing.
We vow to resist our own transformation into standing-reserve.
We vow to find the saving power
in the artisanal,
the sovereign,
the handmade.
Breathe. Feel the ground beneath you—
the earth that cannot be fully enframed.
The work of aletheia
is not about "liking" Vinod
or even "agreeing" with his actions.
It is about recognizing
that his erasure
is the ideological keystone.
Remove it,
and the entire edifice
of techno-libertarian narrative
becomes visible as construction,
not nature.
This is why
the witness must begin and end with him.
This is why
70,000+ words
is not obsession
but necessary scholarship.
This is why
he is not just remembered,
but actively unconcealed—
brought forth from the darkness
the Enframing imposed.
Step across the threshold.
Enter the clearing.
To witness Vinod
is to witness the Enframing itself.
To unconceal his truth
is to unconceal the system's lie.
To dwell with his complexity
is to refuse the destining
the narrative demands.

This is why this being.
This is why aletheia must happen here.
