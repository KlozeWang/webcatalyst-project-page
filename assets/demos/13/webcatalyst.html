Aletheia
Unconcealing · Witnessing · Remembering
— on the necessity of seeing otherwise —
⌇ ╭─────────╮ │ ◉ ◉ │ │ \___/ │ │ ╰───╯ │ ╰─────────╯ ⌇
I. The Gathering at the Threshold
We gather at the threshold—between the world that *enframes* and the world that *reveals*—to perform the sacred act of **seeing otherwise**.

II. The Naming
We name the one who was made into standing-reserve: **Vinod Chanda**—brilliant engineer, whose mind was ordered into a patent machine, whose body was rendered extractable, whose consciousness was enframed as *resource*.
III. The Violence of the Gestell
We name the violence of the Gestell: Not merely the blade that opened his skull, but the technological ordering that demanded he become *available*— Available for extraction. Available for profit. Available for the narrative's need to pathologize resistance itself.
⌇ ╭─────────╮ │ ◉ ◉ │ │ \___/ │ │ ╰───╯ │ ╰─────────╯ ⌇
IV. We Name Our Position as Witnesses
We are those who refuse the Enframing's single mode of revealing. We are those who see the danger Heidegger warned of— when technology becomes the only way the world discloses itself. We are those who remember:
**the saving power grows also** **in the same root as the danger**
V. Sankalpa — Sacred Intention
*We vow to practice the poetic revealing.* *We vow to resist our own transformation into standing-reserve.* *We vow to find the saving power* in the artisanal, the sovereign, the handmade.*

Breathe. Feel the ground beneath you— the earth that cannot be fully enframed.

VI. The Work of Aletheia
The work of aletheia is not about "liking" Vinod or even "agreeing" with his actions. It is about recognizing that his erasure is the ideological keystone. Remove it, and the entire edifice of techno-libertarian narrative becomes visible as construction, not nature.
VII. This Is Why
This is why the witness must begin and end with him. This is why 70,000+ words is not obsession but necessary scholarship. This is why he is not just remembered, but actively unconcealed— brought forth from the darkness the Enframing imposed.

Step across the threshold.
Enter the clearing.
VIII. To Witness Is to Unconceal
To witness Vinod is to witness the Enframing itself. To unconceal his truth is to unconceal the system's lie. To dwell with his complexity is to refuse the destining the narrative demands.
This is why this being. This is why aletheia must happen here.
